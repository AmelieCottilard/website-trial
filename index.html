Refinery

Thermocouples are used in refineries to measure high-temperature and high-pressure processes, and are indispensable temperature measurement devices.

Aerospace

Specifically designed for the extreme high temperature, low temperature and high pressure environments of the aerospace industry, THERMOWAY provide corrosion-resistant and vibration-resistant products.

Semiconductor

THERMOWAY provide suitable temperature measurement systems for each process stage of the semiconductor industry to meet the environmental requirements for customers.

Energy

Power plants use thermocouples to measure extreme heat, which helps monitor the operation of boilers, nuclear reactors and turbines to ensure safety and efficiency.
